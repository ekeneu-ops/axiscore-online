Read this before drawing month-to-month conclusions: this is a cash-flow view — what moved through the bank each month. The monthly Net swings hard (May +$83K, June −$56K) because vendor payments are lumpy and are booked in the month paid, not the month the sales happened. So a big month here means few vendor bills were paid that month, not that it was more profitable. "COGS (vendor cash)" likewise is cash paid (it includes paying down pre-2026 vendor debt and non-Amazon/ISG vendors), so it runs higher than true cost-of-sales. For real monthly profitability (gross revenue − accrual COGS − sourced fees), use the annual accrual anchor below; a true monthly accrual split needs warehouse queries (flagged, not smoothed). The reliable monthly takeaways here are Revenue collected, Overhead, and Net cash change.
