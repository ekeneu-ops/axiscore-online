Feed: ../data/net_position.json — published by the AWS finance read-runner on the daily pipeline cadence (EventBridge, 07:00 UTC). No Mac Mini / launchd / refresh.sh in the runtime path.
Vendor AP is the AP app's own header figure “Net after credits” — the buckets it will actually let you pay (Pay + Review + Wait + Unmatched) less open vendor credits, with vendor-settled invoices and parked FedEx freight excluded. It is published to finance/ap/data.json as totals.net_after_credits by spine-ap-refresh (read-only finance_runner over ap.v_outstanding + ap.v_open_credits), so this line moves with the app. ISG dues are inside this line (rollup ISG) — there is no separate ISG card.
The static AP Scrutiny mirror still shows the older statement + recent gross accrual (the sticky 6/3 statement plus every invoice since, which never decreases when invoices are paid) and therefore reads higher. Same file, different definition — not a broken feed. Not included in Net after credits: invoices dated before 2026-01-01, and the off-statement manual items (ZOLL K4080, Veritiv).
